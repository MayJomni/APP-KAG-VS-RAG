Machine Learning in Healthcare: A Comprehensive Review

Abstract:
This paper reviews the application of machine learning (ML) techniques in healthcare diagnostics.
We analyze 150 studies published between 2018 and 2024.

Introduction:
Machine learning has revolutionized medical diagnosis. Deep learning models achieve 94.2% accuracy
in cancer detection. Neural networks outperform radiologists in chest X-ray analysis by 12%.

Key Findings:
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) detect diabetic retinopathy with 97% sensitivity.
- Random Forest models predict heart failure 30 days before clinical symptoms.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) extracts patient data from 10,000 medical records per hour.
- Reinforcement learning optimizes drug dosing in ICU patients, reducing mortality by 18%.

Models Used:
ResNet-50 was trained on 500,000 chest X-rays from 3 hospitals.
BERT model fine-tuned on 2 million clinical notes achieves F1-score of 0.91.
XGBoost classifier uses 47 patient features to predict readmission risk.
