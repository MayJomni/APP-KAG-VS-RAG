REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL - ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE SOLUTION

SECTION 1 - TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
1. The system must support a minimum of 10,000 concurrent users.
2. Response time shall not exceed 2 seconds for all standard operations.
3. The solution must be compatible with Windows Server 2019 and Linux Ubuntu 20.04.
4. The vendor shall provide a REST API with full OpenAPI 3.0 documentation.
5. Data encryption must use AES-256 for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit.
6. The system should support multi-factor authentication (MFA).
7. Backup and recovery must be completed within 4 hours (RTO) and 1 hour (RPO).

SECTION 2 - LEGAL AND COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
1. The selected vendor must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) within 5 business days.
2. The solution must comply with GDPR and local data protection laws.
3. Vendor must provide proof of ISO 27001 certification.
4. All source code must be held in escrow with a licensed third party.
5. The vendor shall carry a minimum liability insurance of USD 5 million.

SECTION 3 - FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS
1. The vendor must provide a fixed-price proposal with no hidden costs.
2. Payment terms shall be Net 30 days from invoice receipt.
3. Vendor should provide a 3-year total cost of ownership (TCO) analysis.
4. Discount structure for multi-year contracts is required.
5. The vendor must accept bank guarantee as financial security.

SECTION 4 - ADMINISTRATIVE REQUIREMENTS
1. Vendor must have at least 5 years of experience in similar projects.
2. The project manager must hold a PMP certification.
3. Vendor should provide 24/7 technical support with a 4-hour SLA.
4. Monthly progress reports must be submitted to the client.
5. The vendor must assign a dedicated account manager for this project.
